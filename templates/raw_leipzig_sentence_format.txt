Expected raw Leipzig sentence format
===================================

One sentence per line.
Typical format:

<sentence_id>\t<sentence_text>

Example:

1	This is the first sentence.
2	This is the second sentence.

Recommended Drive location:

data/raw/{language_code}/{language_code}_sentences.txt

Examples:

data/raw/en/en_sentences.txt
data/raw/fr/fr_sentences.txt
data/raw/es/es_sentences.txt
data/raw/de/de_sentences.txt
data/raw/cs/cs_sentences.txt
