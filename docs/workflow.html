Provisional candidate material — not validated CEFR data.
Current automated outputs are model-generated candidate material for expert review. They are not validated CEFR vocabulary data, not approved Atlas releases, and must not be cited as validated findings.

Five-stage workflow

Stage 1: Corpus preparation

Prepare and document corpus inputs.

Stage 2: Processing

Tokenise, POS-tag, lemmatise and compute statistics.

Stage 3: Sampling

Select candidate lemmas and sentences.

Stage 4: Function tagging

Run Pass 1, Pass 2 informed validator pass and Pass 3 automated resolution pass.

Stage 5: QA and expert review

Prepare outputs for expert review.

Pass terminology

PassCorrect termExplanation
Pass 1Initial model tagging passAssigns an initial sentence-level function.
Pass 2Informed validator passReviews Pass 1 output; not independent blind double coding.
Pass 3Automated resolution passAutomated model resolution; not human adjudication.

Illustrative example

Could you send me the report by Friday? may be tagged as requesting action, then reviewed by the informed validator pass, optionally processed by automated resolution and sent for expert review. The row remains provisional until reviewed, validated and approved.
