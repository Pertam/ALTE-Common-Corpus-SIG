Worked example: one sentence through the workflow

The example below shows how one sentence moves through the pilot workflow. The labels shown here are illustrative only and are not validated CEFR data.

Stage What happens Example output
Stage 1: Corpus preparation The sentence is identified in the source corpus and assigned a sentence ID. Could you send me the report by Friday?
Stage 2: Processing and lemma statistics The sentence is tokenised, lemmatised and linked to corpus statistics. Target lemma: send
Stage 3: Sampling The lemma passes the ARF threshold and the sentence is selected for tagging. Sample sentence retained for LLM tagging.
Stage 4: LLM function tagging The whole sentence is tagged for communicative function using the controlled taxonomy. Illustrative function: requesting someone to do something
Stage 5: QA and expert review The output is checked, exported, and reviewed by experts. Status: provisional Tier 4 candidate material

Stage 1: Corpus preparation

Collect raw corpus files, metadata, licensing notes, and source documentation. Clean and normalise corpus text.

Stage 2: Processing and lemma statistics

Tokenise, sentence-split, POS-tag, lemmatise, filter content words, and calculate raw frequency, frequency per million, dispersion, ARF and ARF per million.

Stage 3: Sampling

Filter to content words and ARF per million ≥ 50. Randomly sample candidate lemmas and extract sentence examples.

Stage 4: LLM function tagging

Run Pass 1 tagging, Pass 2 blind validation, and Pass 3 lemma-level aggregation using the controlled taxonomy.

Stage 5: QA and expert review

Run validation checks, export review workbooks, collect expert review, and record adjudication decisions.
