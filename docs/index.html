Project overview

The pilot tests how corpus evidence, lexical senses, language-specific forms, sentence-level communicative functions and expert judgement can be combined across English, French, Spanish, German and Czech.

The fundamental data unit is one target lemma occurrence in one sentence. Each row receives two independent annotations: the sense of the target lemma and the communicative function of the whole sentence.

Sense and function are different

SentenceTargetLexical senseSentence function

The rule applies to all applicants.applybe relevant or validstating a rule or condition

Sense belongs to the target lemma; function belongs to the sentence. They are labelled independently and combined only for review and analysis.

Methodology

Current workflow

  1. Corpus preparation
  2. Processing and lemma statistics
  3. Target-occurrence sampling
  4. Human-approved lexical-sense inventories and sense tagging
  5. Independent communicative-function tagging
  6. Combined QA, expert review and sense-level aggregation
