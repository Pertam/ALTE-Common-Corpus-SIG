Why this project matters

Existing multilingual vocabulary resources often tell us which words occur, but not clearly which sense of a word is being used, what the surrounding sentence is doing communicatively, or how those patterns differ across languages.

This pilot tests whether corpus evidence, lexical-sense analysis, communicative-function tagging and expert judgement can be combined into a transparent multilingual resource. The aim is to produce sense-specific functional profiles rather than treating every use of a headword as though it meant the same thing.

What the pilot is building

The pilot covers English, French, Spanish, German and Czech. Its fundamental data unit is one target lemma occurrence in one sentence.

Each row records two distinct but related judgements:

  • Lexical sense: what the target lemma means in that sentence.
  • Communicative function: what the whole sentence is doing.

Sense and function are different

SentenceTargetLexical senseSentence function
Apply the cream twice daily.applyput a substance onto a surfacegiving an instruction
The rule applies to all applicants.applybe relevant or validstating a rule or condition

Sense belongs to the target lemma; function belongs to the sentence. Pass 1 produces them as separate decisions. Pass 2 then performs an informed critical review of both while still recording a separate final decision for sense and function.
