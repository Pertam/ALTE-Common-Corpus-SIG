Core methodological commitments

  • The unit of analysis is the concept or sense, not the English word.
  • CEFR levels are function-anchored, not frequency-anchored.
  • Translation equivalents may legitimately differ across languages.
  • Receptive and productive CEFR levels should be separated where evidence permits.
  • LLM-generated outputs are Tier 4 candidate material.
  • Unsupported evidence must not be invented.
  • Human expert review is required before any validation claim.

Scope

The current pilot covers English, French, Spanish, German, and Czech. The aim is to test the methodology before any wider expansion.
