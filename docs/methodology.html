Methodology

Principles governing target occurrences, lexical senses, sentence functions and multilingual alignment.

1. Fundamental unit

The operational row is a target lemma occurrence in a corpus sentence. The lexical layer asks which sense the target occurrence realises; the pragmatic layer asks what the whole sentence is doing.

2. Sense and function are independent

The sense label must not be inferred from a function label, and the function label must not be inferred from the lemma sense. Separate prompts and outputs make their relationship an empirical result rather than a built-in assumption.

3. Sense inventories

For each language + lemma + POS, sampled sentences are considered together. AI proposes a small coarse inventory; a language expert merges, splits, removes, renames or adds senses and marks the retained inventory approved before bulk tagging.

4. Sense-splitting rule

Create a separate sense only where the distinction could materially affect translation, grammatical construction, learner understanding, CEFR judgement or pedagogical treatment. Prefer defensible broad senses over dictionary micro-senses. Every inventory includes OTHER and UNCLEAR.

5. Independent passes

Sense Pass 2 sees the original sentence and approved inventory, but not Pass 1. Function Pass 2 sees the original sentence and taxonomy, but not Pass 1. Agreement is evidence of consistency, not proof of correctness.

6. Multilingual analysis

Inventories are developed independently within each language. English senses are not translated and imposed on other languages. Reviewed senses may later be aligned to broader concept IDs, preserving one-to-many and constructional relationships.

7. Corpus limitations

Sense and function distributions describe the selected corpus. A news-heavy corpus will overrepresent reporting and description and underrepresent interactional functions; corpus composition must therefore accompany interpretation.

8. Validation

  • Measure model–model, model–human and human–human agreement.
  • Inspect confusion matrices, OTHER/UNCLEAR rates and inventory merge/split rates.
  • Review disagreements and a random sample of agreements.
  • Compare lemma-level and sense-level functional profiles.
  • Report results by language, register and POS.
