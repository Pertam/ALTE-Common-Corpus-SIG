Provisional candidate material — not validated CEFR data.
Current automated outputs are model-generated candidate material for expert review. They are not validated CEFR vocabulary data, not approved Atlas releases, and must not be cited as validated findings.

Pilot dispersion-adjusted frequency measure

The implementation uses a transparent pilot dispersion-adjusted frequency measure. It supports engineering and feasibility testing, requires methodological validation, and thresholds based on it must not be interpreted as validated CEFR or corpus-frequency boundaries. Lemma, sense, concept and function

  • Lemma: dictionary-like form observed in corpus processing.
  • Sense/concept: meaning distinction requiring expert/methodological treatment.
  • Function: what a sentence is doing communicatively.
  • CEFR level: a validation claim not produced by the automated tagging workflow alone.
