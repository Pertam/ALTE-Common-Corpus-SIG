Does this produce validated CEFR vocabulary data?

No. At this stage all LLM-generated outputs are provisional candidate material.

What is ARF?

ARF is a dispersion-adjusted frequency measure used to avoid over-weighting lemmas that occur many times in narrow contexts.

Why sentence-level function tagging?

The current task is to identify what the whole sentence is doing communicatively, not simply the topic of the sentence or the meaning of the sampled lemma.

What does Tier 4 mean?

Tier 4 means the assignment is based on LLM-assisted or expert candidate generation without independent corroborating source evidence. It must be flagged for later validation.
