Safe-working principles

  • Do not edit master files directly; use assigned working copies.
  • Preserve row IDs, sentence IDs and taxonomy IDs.
  • Use evidence-status terms: raw, processed, sampled, model-generated, automated-QA-passed, expert-reviewed, adjudication-required, adjudicated, corpus-validated, cross-language-validated, externally validated and approved for publication.
  • Use dates, language codes and status words in filenames.
