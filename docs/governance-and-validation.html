Evidence-status framework

StatusMeaning
rawOriginal corpus input before project processing.
processedTokenised, POS-tagged, lemmatised or otherwise transformed.
sampledSelected for review or tagging by documented criteria.
model-generatedProduced by an LLM; not necessarily correct.
automated-QA-passedPassed programmatic checks; not expert-reviewed.
expert-reviewedChecked by a human expert; not automatically CEFR-validated.
adjudication-requiredNeeds human resolution of disagreement or uncertainty.
adjudicatedResolved by documented human process; not automatically approved.
corpus-validatedCorpus and processing evidence checked.
cross-language-validatedComparable evidence checked across languages.
externally validatedValidated against external criteria or independent evidence.
approved for publicationReleased by authorised governance process.

Validation meanings

Corpus validation checks source suitability and processing. Cross-language validation checks comparability across pilot languages. CEFR-related validation requires evidence connecting outputs to CEFR interpretation; model labels alone are insufficient.
