Provisional candidate material — not validated CEFR data.
Current automated outputs are model-generated candidate material for expert review. They are not validated CEFR vocabulary data, not approved Atlas releases, and must not be cited as validated findings.

Why this work is being undertaken

Vocabulary evidence for assessment cannot be reduced to a translated word list. The pilot investigates corpus frequency, dispersion, examples, communicative function and expert judgement for future multilingual CEFR-related vocabulary work.

Methodological principle Frequency evidence is useful, but CEFR claims require expert and validation evidence.

Use cases after validation

  • Vocabulary evidence for assessment and curriculum discussions.
  • Cross-language comparison of concepts, senses, forms and functions.
  • Research into corpus-informed CEFR vocabulary profiling.

These are intended uses, not claims that approved outputs already exist.
